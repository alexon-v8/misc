Mental Vedic Choreography

a² = (a + d)(a − d) + d²
Why does this always work?

Algebraically, (a + d)(a − d) = a² − d². Therefore, adding d² back recovers a² perfectly!

By picking d so that (a + d) or (a − d) hits a round decade (like 100 or 50), multi-digit multiplication reduces to a single-digit mental calculation plus adding d².
