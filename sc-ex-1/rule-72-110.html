Why 72 instead of 69.3?

Exact compound continuous doubling is 100 × ln(2) ≈ 69.3%. However, 69.3 is difficult to divide mentally and applies to continuous growth. For periodic compound intervals (e.g. 6% to 10%), the actual factor rises to ~71-73. 72 is chosen because it possesses 12 whole divisors, making exhibition mental arithmetic instantaneous!
